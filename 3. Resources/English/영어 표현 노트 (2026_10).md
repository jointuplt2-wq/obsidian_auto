### 🎯 오늘의 복습 퀴즈 (5/7 정답)
> 📚 **목요일(10/1) 복습을 금요일에** — 지난 복습 5.5 → 오늘 **5**

| 틀린 문장 | 내 답 | 정답 |
|---|---|---|
| It **hasn't** the engine. | "dosen't have" 🔶 (철자 + an 누락) | It **doesn't have an** engine. |
| I have been **built** it for four days. | "building" ✔️ | I have been **building** it for four days. ⏳ |
| (**Are** you good at stickers?) Yes, **I do**. | "Yes, I am." ✔️ | Yes, I **am**. |
| I**'m not decide**. | "I hasn't decided yet" 🔶 (has/have) | I **haven't decided** yet. |
| I will show **the finished Porsche**. | "I will show it ~" 🔶 (사람 누락) | I will show **you** the finished Porsche. |
| I need **holiday**. | "a holiday" ✔️ | I need **a holiday / a break**. |
| I will display **the bottom of F-15**. | "I will display it ~" 🔶 (under 누락) | I will display it **under** the F-15. |

**복습 총평:** 5/7
✅ **완전 정복:** been **building**(3번째 만에!) · **Yes, I am** · **a** holiday
📌 **다음에 또 출제:** ① **I haven't** (I + have!) ② doesn't have **an** ③ show **you** ④ 철자 **doesn't**

> 🔥 **복습 직후 대화에서 전부 정확히 사용!**
> under → "I will put it **under** the F-15" ✔️ / Are → "Yes, I **am** excited" ✔️ / Will → "Yes, I **will** assemble it today" ✔️

## 📝 문법 팁 모음

- **at** + 시각 (at 12:30) / **in** + 기간 (in the morning)
- 목록 마지막엔 **and**: "A, B, **and** C"
- 습관은 현재형: "I **study** English every day" (will 안 쓰)
- 원인/결과: **because of** + 명사 / **because** + 문장 / 결과는 **so**
- **ask + 사람 + to + 동사**: "ask you to teach me"
- 장소: **"I'm in [도시]"** (Here is [도시] ✗)
- 질문의 동사 = 대답의 동사: **Are** you ~? → Yes, I **am**. ⭐
